# CrazyGames — Magnet Caravan (EN)

## Title
Magnet Caravan

## About (short)
Pull scrap with your magnet, grow a caravan tail, and bank it in the Recycler to survive enemy waves. Use a Flip pulse to repel threats and deflect projectiles.

## Full description
Magnet Caravan is a fast top-down arcade survival with short roguelite runs. Your magnet pulls scrap into a growing tail — the longer the caravan, the higher the risk. Enemies chase, shoot, and try to cut your tail apart.

Your lifesaver is Flip: a pulse that pushes enemies away, can deflect shots, and grants a brief invulnerability window. Between waves, pick upgrades to tune your build: stronger magnet, faster Flip, Dash unlocks, shrapnel bursts and more.

Bank your tail in the Recycler Zone: hold position briefly to convert collected scrap into bolts (currency) and heal. Daily mode offers a fixed UTC seed with special modifiers and rules.

## SEO description
Magnet + tail arcade survival: collect scrap, deflect shots with Flip, bank loot in the Recycler, and pick upgrades between waves.

## How to play
- Move (WASD/Arrow keys or virtual joystick).
- Collect scrap to extend your tail.
- Press Flip to repel enemies and deflect projectiles.
- Enter the Recycler Zone and hold to bank: gain bolts and heal.
- After each wave, choose 1 of 3 upgrades.
