# Poki — Magnet Caravan (EN)

## Title
Magnet Caravan

## About (short)
Magnet-pull scrap, grow your tail, and bank it in the Recycler. Flip to repel enemies and survive wave after wave.

## Full description
Magnet Caravan is a snappy top-down arcade survival with roguelite upgrade choices. Collect scrap to extend your caravan tail, then decide when to cash it in for bolts and healing at the Recycler Zone.

Flip is your emergency tool: a pulse that pushes enemies away (and can deflect shots). Between waves, pick upgrades that shape your run. Daily mode adds a fixed daily seed with unique modifiers.

## SEO description
Top-down magnet survival: collect scrap, Flip to survive, bank your tail, pick upgrades between waves.

## How to play
- Move: WASD/Arrow keys or joystick.
- Flip: Space / Flip button.
- Recycler: stay inside to bank your tail.
