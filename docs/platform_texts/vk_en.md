# VK — Magnet Caravan (EN)

## Title
Magnet Caravan

## About (short)
Magnet + tail + enemy waves. Collect scrap, bank your tail at the Recycler, and survive with Flip and upgrades.

## Full description
Magnet Caravan delivers short runs with meaningful upgrade choices and a daily seeded mode. Pull scrap into your caravan tail, but beware: shooters and cutters will try to break it apart. Flip is your clutch tool — repel enemies, deflect shots, and gain a short invulnerability window.

Bank your tail in the Recycler Zone to gain bolts and healing, then continue to the next wave with new upgrades and rarities.

## SEO description
Magnet tail arcade: collect scrap, Flip to survive, bank loot in the Recycler, pick upgrades between waves.

## How to play
- Move: WASD/Arrow keys or joystick
- Flip: Space / Flip button
- Recycler Zone: hold position to bank your tail
