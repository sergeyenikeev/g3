# Universal Portal Copy — Magnet Caravan (EN)

## Title
Magnet Caravan

## About (short)
Collect scrap with a magnet, grow a caravan tail, bank it at the recycler, and survive enemy waves with a lifesaving Flip pulse.

## Full description
Magnet Caravan is a top-down arcade survival game built around short runs, readable controls, and between-wave build choices. Your magnet pulls scrap into a growing caravan tail: the longer it gets, the more valuable your haul becomes, but the risk rises with every new enemy wave.

Flip is your clutch tool. It pushes enemies away, can deflect incoming shots, and opens a brief survival window when things go wrong. Bank your tail in the Recycler Zone for bolts and healing, then pick upgrades between waves to shape each run around your preferred playstyle.

The game also includes a Daily mode with a fixed seed and rotating modifiers.

## SEO description
Top-down magnet survival: collect scrap, Flip to deflect shots, bank your caravan tail, and choose upgrades between waves.

## How to play
- Move with WASD, arrow keys, or the virtual joystick.
- Collect scrap to grow your tail.
- Press Flip to repel enemies and deflect shots.
- Stay in the Recycler Zone until banking is complete.
- Choose upgrades between waves and adapt your build.
